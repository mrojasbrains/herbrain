01So what even is the brain?

The brain is the control center of your whole body. It takes in information from your senses, figures out what it means, decides what to do, and sends out commands, all in a fraction of a second. It is also where your thoughts, memories, emotions and personality live.

The boss

Every movement, thought and feeling starts here. Your brain sends signals through nerves to every part of your body.

Part of the CNS

The brain + the spinal cord = the central nervous system (CNS). All the nerves branching out to the rest of your body are the peripheral nervous system (PNS).

Never off

Even when you are asleep, your brain keeps your heart beating, your lungs breathing and your body temperature steady. Unpaid overtime, every night.

03Brain cells 101

Your brain is made of two main types of cells: neurons, which send messages using electrical and chemical signals, and glial cells, the support crew that feeds, protects and cleans up after the neurons.

  • Dendrites receive messages from other neurons.
  • The cell body has the nucleus and keeps the neuron alive.
  • The axon sends the message onward, like a long cable.
  • Myelin wraps the axon in fat so signals zoom faster.
  • The tiny gap between two neurons is a synapse. Chemicals called neurotransmitters carry the message across.

Gray matter

Mostly neuron cell bodies. It makes up the outer layer of the brain (the cerebral cortex), where most of the thinking happens.

White matter

Bundles of axons wrapped in white, fatty myelin. It sits underneath and works like the brain's wiring, connecting different areas.

Gyri & sulci

The bumps are gyri (one is a gyrus) and the grooves are sulci (one is a sulcus). The folding packs a huge surface into your skull.

04Protected like a VIP

Your brain is soft (think firm tofu), so your body guards it with layers of security.

Skull

The cranium is a helmet made of 8 bones fused together.

Meninges

Three protective layers between skull and brain. From outside in: dura mater, arachnoid mater, pia mater. Remember it as DAP.

Cerebrospinal fluid

CSF is a clear liquid the brain floats in. It cushions bumps and makes the brain feel way lighter than it is.

Blood-brain barrier

A super picky filter in the brain's blood vessels that only lets certain things through. Gatekeeping, but make it healthy.
